## Photographs — Pexels License

Fifteen frames from fifteen Pexels photographs by one photographer, Reborn Filmes, from two
sessions (a bridal getting-ready session and a makeup-studio session), downloaded into the
project (never hot-linked), then cropped, graded once for the whole set and exported for their
frames. Only the processed frames are in this repository. They are sample imagery: nobody shown
works at, is a client of or endorses the (fictional) studio, none of it is the studio's work,
and the page labels it "Sample imagery — illustrative makeup inspiration". Full details and
crops: `PHOTOS.md`.
